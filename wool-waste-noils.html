Wool Waste / Noils.

Short fibres and wool wastes from combing and processing.

Product Overview

Noils are the short fibres combed out when wool is prepared for worsted spinning. Together with laps and other wool wastes from mills, they're valuable raw material for woollen spinning and blends.

  • Noils, laps and hard wastes from wool processing.
  • Good value for woollen spinning, blends and felts.
  • A second life for fibre that would otherwise be wasted.
